# Northwind Appliances – Customer Support (sample)

## Opening hours
Customer support is available Monday to Saturday from 8 am to 8 pm, and on Sunday from 10 am to 4 pm. Support is closed on national public holidays.

## Contact channels
- Phone: the toll-free number printed on the invoice.
- Chat: the chat window on the Northwind website, during opening hours.
- Email: replies within one working day.

## Installation
Free installation is included for refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners. The installation visit is booked automatically after delivery and normally takes place within 48 hours. Wall-mounting kits for air conditioners are charged separately.

## Service requests outside warranty
Repairs outside the warranty period are charged a fixed visit fee of 350 rupees plus the cost of parts. The visit fee is waived if the customer goes ahead with the repair.
