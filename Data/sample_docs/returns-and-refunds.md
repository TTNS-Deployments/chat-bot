# Northwind Appliances – Returns and Refunds (sample)

## Return window
Unused products in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Products that have been installed or used can be returned within 7 days only if they are faulty.

## How to return a product
1. Start the return from the "My orders" page or by calling customer support.
2. Choose a pickup slot. Pickup is free for faulty products and costs a flat 499 rupees for other returns.
3. Keep the product in its original packaging with all accessories and the invoice.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the product passes the quality check at the warehouse. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer; customers are asked for their bank details when the return is approved.

## Exchanges
Instead of a refund, customers can exchange a product for a different model. If the new model costs more, the customer pays the difference; if it costs less, the difference is refunded.
