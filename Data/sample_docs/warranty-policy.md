# Northwind Appliances – Warranty Policy (sample)

## Standard warranty
Every Northwind appliance comes with a two-year standard warranty from the date of delivery. The standard warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, including the motor, compressor, control board and heating elements.

## What the warranty does not cover
The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, power surges, incorrect installation, or repairs carried out by anyone other than a Northwind authorised service partner. Consumable parts such as filters, light bulbs and seals are not covered after the first 90 days.

## Extended warranty
Customers can buy the Northwind Care Plus extended warranty within 60 days of delivery. Care Plus adds three more years of cover, so the total cover is five years, and includes one free annual maintenance visit. Care Plus costs 8 percent of the product price and can be transferred once if the appliance is sold.

## Making a warranty claim
To make a claim, contact customer support with the order number and a description of the fault. A technician visit is normally scheduled within three working days in metropolitan areas and within five working days elsewhere. Repairs under warranty are free of charge, including parts and labour. If an appliance cannot be repaired, Northwind replaces it with the same or an equivalent model.
